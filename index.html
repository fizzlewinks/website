About

I'm Bach, a photographer based in Wellington, New Zealand, working primarily in documentary and editorial photography.

I started out shooting landscapes for fun, but have since found my passion in capturing the stories behind people and events. I enjoy working across a variety of subjects, including music photography and photojournalism. My work has been recognised in the NZAPA, including a Silver award in Photojournalism and the Under 18 category win in 2026.

Available for assignments and editorial work - get in touch via email or Instagram for inquiries and collaborations.
